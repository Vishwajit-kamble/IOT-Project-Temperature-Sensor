About Project & Team

Project Details

Problem: Environmental conditions need continuous monitoring, but manual measurements do not provide convenient real-time visibility or historical analysis.

Solution: An ESP32 and DHT11 sensor collect environmental measurements. A Python-based monitoring application displays real-time values, stores historical readings, and estimates short-term temperature trends from recorded data.
